Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Youropp Africa ("we", "us", "our") operates the Youropp Africa mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Youropp Africa:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (securely hashed)
  • Phone number (optional) — for account recovery
  • Saved and liked opportunities — to personalize your experience
  • Comments you post on opportunities
  • Basic device information — for compatibility and diagnostics

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the service
  • To personalize your experience (saved items, recommendations)
  • To communicate with you about opportunities, updates, and support
  • To ensure the security and integrity of the platform

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication). All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not store payment information.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with:

  • Firebase (Google) — for authentication and database services
  • Legal authorities — when required by law

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and all associated data (Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete account)
  • Opt out of notifications at any time

6. Children's Privacy

Youropp Africa is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

8. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@youroppafrica.com.
